Model appointment booking for a clinic.

Doctors offer time slots. A patient books a free slot for an appointment with a reason. An appointment is booked, then either attended, cancelled or a no show. A patient may cancel up to 24 hours before the start; after that only the reception can cancel. A slot holds at most one appointment that is not cancelled.

Receptionists book and cancel for any patient and mark attendance. Doctors see the appointments of their own slots. Patients see only their own appointments. When an appointment is booked or cancelled, the patient is told. Patients book through a web page that lists the free slots of the next two weeks.
