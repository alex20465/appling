Model a lending library.

Members borrow copies of books. A book has a title, an author and an ISBN that is unique; the library owns several copies of a book. A loan runs 14 days and a member may have at most three open loans. A loan is open until the copy comes back; a loan that is not back after its due date is overdue, and the member then pays a fine of 0.50 per day. Members with an unpaid fine cannot borrow.

Librarians lend and take back copies and waive fines. Members see only their own loans and fines. Every night, overdue loans are marked overdue. Members find books through a public catalog that lists the books with how many copies are available.
