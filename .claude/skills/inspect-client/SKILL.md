# Inspector client

Internal flows of the read only inspector client that are not visible from one file.

## Snapshot and selection

The canvas, the inspector and the selection read only the snapshot, which the server builds from disk and pushes over `/events` after every change (100ms debounce of the file watcher). The client never writes a model file; the only write is `PUT /api/view` for the canvas settings. Selection is client state: a node click, a link in the inspector, an `ide_act` of an agent session, a host's panel action or the cursor of a `.appling` editor sent by the host set it, and **×** on the inspector clears it. A selected sub-anchor address (`sales.Checkout#step[2]`) shows its owning element. Source: `App.tsx`, `Inspector.tsx`, `server.ts`.

## The pyramid

`growTree` (`packages/viz/src/views.ts`) rebuilds the whole tree from the graph and the `unfolded` id set on every change; nothing else holds tree state. Only `ViewSettings` (`lens`, the entry level, and `linkEntities`) persist, through `/api/view` in `.appling/view.json`, merged over `DEFAULT_VIEW` on load; the unfolded set and the camera do not. The levels are the keys of `LENSES` in order, L1 `domain` to L5 `access`; `levelOf(kind)` gives a kind's level, none for scenarios and migrations, which the pyramid leaves out. Node ids are paths: the seed `~` (in the tree, never drawn), a module `~/<module>`, an element `<parent>/<qn>`. There are no kind groups. A module unfolds with its id in `unfolded`, a side of an element with `sideId(id, 'up' | 'down')` (`<id>^up`). An element of the entry level has both sides; what unfolded from a side keeps that side (`TreeNode.side`), so a branch never turns around. A declaration is drawn exactly once: `elements()` marks all siblings as drawn before anything unfolds from the first, so the nearer occurrence is the drawn one. When an unfolded side also leads to a declaration that is drawn elsewhere already, `growTree` leaves it out of `children` and lists its qualified name in `TreeNode.links`; `joins(placed)` (`canvas/plant.ts`) gives one trace per link, routed like every other trace, behind the chips and lit when either end is the current node. `TreeNode.open` lists the unfolded sides, since a side can be open with only links. Folding a side deletes its `sideId` and every id under its children. Source: `views.ts`, `canvas/ModelCanvas.tsx`.

Children come from `linked(index, qn, side)`: every declaration with a level that shares a non `contains` edge with the element, in either direction, lifted to top level declarations, split by higher (`up`) or lower (`down`) level, minus link entities unless `linkEntities` is on. The direction of the edge does not matter, which is how an action leads up to the role its policy names. For L5, `links()` also derives the pages and interfaces that expose what the policies of a role, actor or tenant name (an actor through its roles, a query through its entity), and `linked` hides below an L5 element what one of those L4 exposures already links to. Relations inside one level never unfold: `peers(index, qn)` gives them and the canvas draws the drawn ones with a dashed outline (`.related`) while the element is selected. Children are sorted by level (the nearest first), then kind rank (`KIND_ORDER`), then `index.relevanceOf`, then qualified name (never by source order).

`layoutTree(root, entry)` (`layout.ts`) places the bands, L5 on top, the entry band at y 0. A module is a frame around its entry elements (`Placed.width`, minus `FRAME`), drawn as a chip while folded. Every element owns the strip above and below it, as wide as its wider side, and stands in the middle of it. Per side and level, unfolded children get strips of their own and the folded ones wrap into a grid (two or fewer in one row, else about as many columns as rows), so a band grows by rows. A chip in a grid never leaves its row or its place in it: what it unfolds stands in a strip beside the grid. `route` gives the rows their y and stores the bands on the seed (`Placed.bands`); `bandsOf(placed)` reads them for the rects behind the nodes and the level rail. A slot (`SLOT`) holds the whole chip (`WIDE` and `HALF` in `ModelCanvas.tsx` must stay inside it, and texts are clipped to fit). So unfolding moves the nodes beside it aside and can make a band taller; the order, the row and the place in it are stable, the distances are not.

`route` (`layout.ts`, called by `layoutTree`) routes every trace and stores on each `Placed` its `route` (from the element it unfolded from) and its `joins` (`TreeNode.links`). All that an element leads to on one side is one net: it leaves the middle of that edge of the chip, runs on a track of its own in the channel before each row it ends in, ends at a place of its own on the edge of each chip (pins) and passes a row through a gap between chips. No two nets share a stretch and they cross at right angles only. `ordered` and `cost` order the tracks of a channel for the fewest crossings, every net is rerouted to the way that crosses the fewest others, and a channel is as high as its tracks need, so rows and bands move apart with their traces. `traces(placed, radius)` and `joins(placed)` (`canvas/plant.ts`) only cut the corners at 45 degrees; every trace has `POINTS` (32) points, so the CSS `d` transition moves one into another. A trace takes the level colour of the element they start from; the hovered lineage and a selected node's connections are brighter and thicker, the rest fades. `views.test.ts` checks that unfolding keeps every row and place, that no trace or join runs through a chip and that no two nets run along the same stretch; crossings are allowed. Nodes are chips of one size (`outline`) with a cell in the layer's colour that holds the node's icon (`ViewSettings.icons`, a Lucide name through `iconOf` of `icons.ts`, written by the `set_icons` MCP tool into `.appling/view.json`, which the server watches and pushes as a `view` event). Source: `layout.ts`, `canvas/plant.ts`, `ModelCanvas.tsx`.

## Pan, zoom and clicks

React registers `wheel` as passive, so the canvas adds its own non passive listener to stop page scroll. A drag starts on pointer down and counts as a pan only after 4px; pointer up clears the drag ref in a `setTimeout`, after the click handler had the chance to read `moved`, so a pan never unfolds a node. A click on a chip selects it (`press`); on a module it also opens or folds its frame. The two tabs of an element are their own click targets (`.bud-button`: the path `TAB` as `.bud-disc`, curving out of the top or bottom edge, and a chevron `.bud-arrow` that turns around while that side is open; `grow(node, side)`) and stop propagation, so a tab never selects. The level rail (`.levels`) is HTML over the SVG: one plain yellow label `L1` to `L5` per level at the left edge, without a background, equal squares centred beside their bands (the entry level in full yellow, the question in a tooltip on hover); `RAIL` keeps the fitted view right of it. The inspector is a grid column of `.canvas-area` (`--inspector` wide), not an overlay, so the canvas shrinks while it is open. The line of the current node stands in the bottom bar; there is nothing at the top of the canvas. Stems are drawn through the CSS `d` property (`style={{ d: 'path(...)' }}`, Chromium only). A new node and its stem grow out of the parent's edge together: `stem-grow` starts from the collapsed path in `--seedling`, `sprout` from `--from-x`/`--from-y`, both with `--spring` and the delay of `sproutDelays` (`canvas/plant.ts`), so keep their timing equal or the node leaves its trace; the same holds for `--settle` on moved nodes and traces. Nothing animates forever (the change halo and the lit path are static), so an idle canvas never repaints. `modelPulse` (`views.ts`) finds the drawn elements a new index added or changed for the waves; `canvas/sound.ts` synthesizes the sounds (Web Audio, started only by a press, switched off in `localStorage`). `follow` frames a node and its new children after the layout placed them, zooming out only when they do not fit. Source: `ModelCanvas.tsx`, `canvas.css`, `packages/inspect/src/client/styles.css`.

## Relevance

`GraphIndex` computes `relevance(graph)` once from `@appling/model/relevance` (the subpath keeps langium out of the browser bundle) and `relevanceOf(qn)` lifts members to their declaration. The pyramid orders the declarations of a kind by the score and maps `relevanceLevel(score)` to the `core`/`supporting`/`peripheral` class for emphasis. Source: `packages/model/src/relevance.ts`, `views.ts`, `ModelCanvas.tsx`.
